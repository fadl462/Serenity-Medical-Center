How Serenity approaches personal information.

PRIVACY

A clear approach to personal information.

Serenity Medical Center aims to handle patient and visitor information responsibly, transparently and only for legitimate healthcare, communication and operational purposes.

Information we may receive

Information may be provided when you contact Serenity, request an appointment, submit an enquiry or otherwise interact with the website. This may include your name, contact details, appointment preferences and information you choose to include in a message.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, coordinate requested visits, provide support and improve the patient experience. Sensitive medical information should not be submitted through a general website form unless Serenity specifically instructs you to do so.

Contact

For questions about privacy or how information is handled, contact the Serenity team at Contact Serenity →
