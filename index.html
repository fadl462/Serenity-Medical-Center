01 / ABOUT SERENITY

Medicine built around you.

Serenity Medical Center is a private, patient-centered clinic in Damas, Yaoundé. The center combines quality healthcare, compassion and innovation across general medicine, maternal and child care, diagnostics, surgery and emergency services.
