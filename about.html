01 · OUR STORY

Built around
the patient.

Serenity Medical Center is a private healthcare facility in Yaoundé, established in 2020 and located at Maison Damas, Chris Complex.

Public profiles describe Serenity as a modern, clean and professional environment where qualified health practitioners provide general consultations, maternal and child care, laboratory tests, ultrasound, minor surgery and preventive care.
