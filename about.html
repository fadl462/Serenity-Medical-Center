OUR STORY

Built around the patient.

Serenity Medical Center was established in 2020 in Yaoundé. Public profiles describe the clinic as a private healthcare facility providing general medicine, maternal and child care, laboratory diagnostics, surgery and emergency services.

We are shaping a healthcare experience where professional treatment and human connection belong in the same room.
