01 · SERVICE OVERVIEW

Care for mothers,
babies & families.

Pregnancy, maternity and early childhood bring important moments where professional guidance and continuity of care matter.

Serenity's maternal and child care service is designed around families, supporting mothers and children through appropriate clinical conversations, assessment and follow-up.

02 · FAMILY CARE

Support for every
important stage.

From pregnancy and maternity support to paediatric care, the focus is on clear guidance and a connected family experience.

01

02

03

04

Paediatric support

Clinical attention for children and families when a child needs assessment or follow-up.
